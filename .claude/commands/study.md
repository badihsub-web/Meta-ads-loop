Monthly learning review.

1. Look at the current biggest leak for each company (newest results.md).
2. Research what's working on Meta now for those leaks: benchmark reports, practitioner teardowns,
   Meta's own guidance, courses and communities Badi gives access to, and competitors' live ads
   (`ads_library_search`). Prefer sources with real data over opinions.
3. Log every useful idea in playbook/learning.md with the link.
4. Turn the best 2-3 into test ideas in playbook/test-ideas.md, each tied to a step it should fix.
5. Tell Badi which ideas you'd test next and why, in a few lines. He approves before anything is tested.
6. Commit.
