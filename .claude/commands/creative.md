Ad copy and creative for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read the round's campaign.md, the company's company.md and content.md, playbook/what-works.md,
   playbook/test-ideas.md, and the lessons about creative.
2. Pick the content: from content.md (uploads or page posts), or say what new piece is needed.
   New pieces are made with the `create-content` skill, never here.
3. Write 2-3 copy options as plain text: primary text (first line carries the hook), headline,
   and the call to action. Name each by the lever it pulls (a specific pain, curiosity, social proof,
   a pattern interrupt, a simple promise, permission) and say in one line why it could work on this
   audience. Include the current best ad as the control.
4. Show each option the way people meet it: the first line as it shows before "...more" on a phone,
   next to the visual's first frame.
5. Note anything that needs checking: a claim, the app's own wording, ad-safe (no famous faces).
6. Wait for Badi's pick. Copy is human psychology: he decides by feel, the test decides after.
7. Write the picked copy, exactly as it will run, into section 3 of campaign.md and the content
   IDs into content.md. Commit.
