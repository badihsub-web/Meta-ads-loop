Review the history of: $ARGUMENTS (a company folder, or "all")

The point is learning: what the past ads and content teach us, and what to do next.

1. Read ACCOUNTS.md and the company's company.md for its ad account. Read only: change nothing in Meta.
2. Pull every campaign with the Meta connector (`date_preset: maximum`): spend, impressions, link clicks,
   results and cost per result. Note the objective of each, and skip campaigns that weren't for
   customers (hiring, other companies' pages): say which you skipped.
3. For the best and worst few, pull the ads and their creatives (`ads_get_creatives`): copy, headline,
   format, hook. Note which angle each one used.
4. Find the patterns: which targeting, objective, format, angle and hook got the cheapest real results,
   and which burned money. Compare like with like (same goal, same country, same currency).
5. Be honest about what the numbers can't say. A cheap WhatsApp chat isn't a sale. If sales per
   campaign aren't recorded, say that this is the gap and ask Badi for them.
6. Write each pattern as a lesson in playbook/lessons.md (low confidence unless it held more than
   once), the best ideas as test ideas, and the angles into the company's content.md.
7. Give Badi the feedback block (Working / Not working / Try next) in a few short lines, plus at most
   one question. Commit.
