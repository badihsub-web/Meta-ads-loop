## Content feedback (2026-10-13, day 1)
- **Hooks in use:** "You're not lazy. Every task just looks urgent." (C-002, C-004) · "Not everything on your list is urgent." (C-003, C-005) · "Your list has 23 things. Only 3 actually matter." (C-007). The relief angle ("you're not lazy") got Meta's money first, but most of its clicks were Audience Network, so it's not proven yet.
- **Mismatch:** every ad says "free trial", but the app is free forever with a $4.99 upgrade. Fine once JJJ's trial ships; until then the ad promises something different from what people get.
- **Untested:** the 3 videos (UGC guy, Sorting game, Podcast) got almost no spend. Not losers, just never shown.

## Content feedback (2026-10-19, week 1: ads Oct 12–17, posts Oct 12–17)
- **Ads, money went to two pieces:** "23 things" static (51% of spend, 14 installs, $4.30) and "You're not lazy" carousel (38%, 9 installs, $4.96). The 3 videos got $4.51 together. Too many ads for $25/day again (L-006).
- **Copy problems still live:** "You're not lazy" and "UGC guy" still say "Start your free trial"; the app has no trial yet. Two creatives ("You're not lazy", "3 Non-Negotiables") have a **Spanish headline** ("Peaches: Lista de tareas IA") on a US English ad. Fix both before the next round.
- **Organic posts:** 6 posts, all 7pm ET. The 2 Reels got 623 of 644 views (97%). The 4 photo/carousel posts got 21 views total (no followers yet, so feed posts reach almost no one). Best: the "Quick game: Non-Negotiable or Flexible?" Reel (433 views FB+IG, 1 save, 7s average watch on IG). Zero follows, zero link clicks from posts.
- **Signal to test:** the game/sorting idea won organically but never got ad money (C-003, $0.25). Give it a fair test as an ad.
